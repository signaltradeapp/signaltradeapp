# Billing

## Plans

| Plan | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Pro, monthly** | $29/month | Unlimited accounts and copy groups, cross-broker copying, risk tools, journal, charts, TradingView webhooks and API access. No per-account fees. |
| **Pro, yearly** | $199/year | The same as monthly Pro, billed once a year. |
| **Self-hosted** | $599 one-time | A ready-to-run deployment on your own server, with lifetime updates. See [Self-hosted](https://signaltradeapp.com/self-hosted). |

Pro is $29 every month from the first month, with no code needed. Current prices: [signaltradeapp.com/pricing](https://signaltradeapp.com/pricing).

There is no free plan. Copying and trading need an active trial or Pro subscription.

## Free trial

- New accounts get a **5-day free trial** with every Pro feature.
- A card is required to start, but you are not charged during the trial.
- When the trial ends, your card is charged and Pro renews automatically: monthly at $29 or yearly at $199, depending on what you chose.
- Cancel before the trial ends and you are not charged.

## Start or upgrade

1. Open **Billing** (also under **Settings → Billing**).
2. Click **Start 5-Day Free Trial**, or **Upgrade to Pro** if you have already used your trial.
3. Enter your payment details. Payments are handled by Stripe.
4. Your trial or plan starts right away.

Pro is paid by card through Stripe. The self-hosted license can be paid by card or crypto.

## Cancel

1. Open **Billing**.
2. Click **Manage Subscription**.
3. Choose **Cancel** in the Stripe portal.

Pro is month-to-month. After you cancel, you keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for, and you are not charged again.

## What happens when a plan ends

When your trial ends without a payment, or a cancelled subscription reaches the end of its period, your account moves to the free tier:

- **Every copy group membership is removed.** Copying stops on all your accounts, as leader and as follower.
- You can no longer place orders through Signal Trade App (chart, webhooks, API).
- **Open positions are not closed.** Anything you hold stays at your broker. Manage it in your broker's platform.
- Your connections and journal stay.

If you subscribe again, set up your copy groups again in the Cockpit and check each follower's ratio and switches before you trade.

## Invoices and payments

- The **Payment History** table on the Billing page lists each payment and trial with date, type, amount and status.
- Invoices and receipts are in the Stripe portal under **Manage Subscription**.
- Refunds are handled case by case. Email support@signaltradeapp.com with your invoice.

## Delete your account

Email support@signaltradeapp.com. Deletion removes your user record, connections, groups and journal data. Some billing records may be kept for legal and tax reasons.
