# Signal Trade App user guide

This guide is for traders who use [Signal Trade App](https://signaltradeapp.com), the cloud trade copier for futures prop firm accounts on Tradovate and Rithmic. It covers everyday use, from your first copied trade to risk tools and billing.

Building something with webhooks or the API? See the [developer docs](../developers/) instead.

## Before you start

- Futures trading carries a substantial risk of loss. Copied trades can fill at different prices than the leader's.
- Your prop firm's rules (max contracts, daily loss limit, drawdown) are enforced by the firm at the broker, not by Signal Trade App.
- Test every new setup on a demo or evaluation account first.
