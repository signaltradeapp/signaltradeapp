# Journal and analytics

The **Journal** imports every closed trade from your connected accounts automatically. There is nothing to upload. It works out P&L, fees, win rate, profit factor, Sharpe ratio, expectancy and max drawdown, and gives you a calendar, notes, tags and CSV export.

Website guide: [Using the trading journal](https://signaltradeapp.com/user-guide/journal).

## Pick an account or a group

The dropdown at the top decides what every tab shows. The Calendar, Analytics, Trades and Accounts tabs all follow it, and so does Export CSV.

| Choice | Shows |
|---|---|
| **All accounts** | Everything combined. |
| **Leader & standalone accounts** | Your own trading. |
| **Follower accounts** | Only the copies. |
| A copy group | Every account in that group. |
| One account | That account only. Accounts are marked leader or follower in the list. |

### Copies count once

When you view several accounts together, a leader trade and its copies count as **one trade** for win rate, trade count, streaks and averages. A trade copied to ten followers is still one decision. P&L, fees and drawdown still add up every account, because that money is real.

## Tabs

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Calendar** | Your trading days with daily P&L, weekly totals and a **Daily Plan & Review** note for each day. Click a day to open its plan and review, with that day's trades listed underneath. |
| **Analytics** | Total P&L, win rate, profit factor, max drawdown, expectancy, Sharpe ratio, average trade, best and worst trades, long vs short results, and charts: daily P&L, cumulative P&L, P&L by symbol, and results by weekday and hour. |
| **Trades** | Every closed trade: account, symbol, side, quantity, entry, exit, gross, commission and net P&L, plus your notes and tags. Each row is marked **own** or **copy**. Filter by date (From / To), symbol and side. |
| **Accounts** | One row per account with trades, win rate, net P&L, fees, max drawdown and best/worst trade. |

## Review a day

1. Open the **Journal** and pick the account or group.
2. On the **Calendar** tab, click the day. Its trades appear under the plan and review.
3. Write your plan or post-session review and click **Save Plan**. If you click another day before saving, the Journal asks before throwing your text away.

## Notes and tags on a trade

1. Go to the **Trades** tab.
2. Click the note button on the trade.
3. Write a note and add tags. Tags you used before are suggested as you type.
4. Save.

## Metrics explained

| Metric | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Win rate | Share of trades that made money. A high win rate with poor reward-to-risk can still lose money. |
| Profit factor | Gross profit divided by gross loss. Above 1 means the winners outweigh the losers. |
| Sharpe ratio | Return relative to how much results swing. Higher is steadier. |
| Expectancy | Average expected profit per trade. |
| Max drawdown | The largest peak-to-trough loss. Keep it well inside your prop firm's limit. |

## Export to CSV

1. Pick the account or group at the top.
2. On the **Trades** tab, set the date range and filters. On the **Calendar** tab, the export covers the month you are viewing.
3. Click **Export CSV**.

You get one row per trade with gross, commission and net P&L. Use it for tax software, payout records or your own spreadsheet.

The Cockpit's Orders panel also has **Export today's trades**.

## Share P&L

**Share P&L** turns your closed trades into an image card with total P&L, win rate, number of trades, profit factor, best trade and an equity curve.

### Open it

| Where | How |
|---|---|
| Cockpit | Click **Share P&L** in the action bar at the top. It opens on **Today**. |
| Journal | On the **Analytics** tab, click **Share P&L** at the top right. It opens on the period and account you are viewing. |

### Choose what it shows

- **Period:** Today, 7D, 30D or All.
- **Account:** all accounts combined, or one account.
- **Hide $ amounts:** shows win rate, trades and profit factor without dollar figures.
- The card **never** shows an account name or number, so your prop account numbers stay private.

The card uses closed trades only. If the period has none yet (common on Today before your first exit), pick 7D, 30D or All.

### Share it

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Download** | Saves the card as a PNG. |
| **Copy image** | Copies the PNG to paste into Discord, X or a chat. |
| **Share** | Opens your phone's share sheet, where supported. Otherwise downloads the image. |
| **Copy share link** | Creates a link to the card with your affiliate code built in, and copies it. |
